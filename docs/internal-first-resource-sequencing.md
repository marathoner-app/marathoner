# Internal-first resource sequencing

- **Status:** Active operating decision
- **Effective:** October 26, 2026
- **Decision owner:** Kevin Tulloch
- **Tracking issue:** [#439](https://github.com/marathoner-app/marathoner/issues/439)
- **Related methodology decision:**
  [Founding-beta methodology review](methodology/founding-beta-review-decision.md)

## Decision

Marathoner will complete and internally validate as much practical product work
as possible before committing material external spending that does not yet
unblock forward momentum.

Material spending has no automatic dollar threshold in this public record. It
means a new legal, professional, platform, research, distribution, or other
outside commitment that the owner considers significant relative to the
project's current resources. Existing tools and explicitly approved,
budget-controlled infrastructure may continue when they are needed for active
development.

This decision changes sequencing, not release standards. It does not authorize
unreviewed prescriptive guidance, bypass Apple distribution requirements,
weaken security or privacy gates, or convert internal evidence into external-
participant evidence.

## Work that should happen first

Before paying an outside dependency, complete every applicable predecessor
that can be done safely with current resources:

- implement the bounded behavior behind an internal or disabled release path;
- exercise it with deterministic fixtures, automated tests, emulators, and
  owner-controlled accounts;
- prove the complete journey on the available web and physical-device
  development surfaces;
- resolve known product, data-integrity, accessibility, and reliability defects
  that could invalidate or materially change the paid work;
- prepare the exact packet, configuration, evidence, or application the outside
  party will receive; and
- record the remaining blocker, expected cost, alternatives, and consequence of
  waiting.

Relationship development, prospective-participant interest, prototype
walkthroughs, and bounded non-prescriptive testing may proceed when their
existing privacy and consent boundaries are followed. They do not authorize
prescriptive participant use.

## Spend activation test

A material external commitment becomes timely only when all of these statements
are true:

1. A named open issue identifies the release or product gate the spending will
   unlock.
2. Applicable no-cost and already-funded predecessor work is complete.
3. The submitted artifact or internally testable behavior is stable enough that
   ordinary implementation work is unlikely to force avoidable repeat spending.
4. The external wait or deliverable is becoming a real critical-path blocker,
   normally within the next four to six weeks of the dependent milestone.
5. The expected cost, scope, alternatives, recurring obligations, and effect of
   delay are recorded.
6. The owner explicitly approves the exact commitment before money is spent or
   work is authorized.

An urgent security, privacy, safety, or data-loss response may require earlier
spending. The responsible action is still to record the exception and its
reason as soon as disclosure is safe.

## Internal testing boundary

Internal readiness may use local services, emulators, fixtures, development and
beta Firebase projects, owner-controlled accounts, development-signed devices,
and disabled or allowlisted feature paths. It may prove implementation without
claiming that an external safety, methodology, legal, platform, or distribution
gate has passed.

If significant spending remains unavailable when an external release gate is
otherwise reached, Marathoner must wait, reduce the release scope, or use an
already documented safe fallback. Schedule pressure does not make an
unapproved or unavailable dependency complete.
