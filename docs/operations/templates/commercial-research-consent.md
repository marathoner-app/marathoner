## Invitation

Marathoner is studying how people preparing for a first marathon respond when
illness, pain signals, missed training, work, family, or another disruption
makes an existing schedule uncertain. You are invited to a research session
about that problem and two fictional product concepts.

Marathoner is a foundation-stage prototype. This session does not provide a
training plan, medical care, diagnosis, injury-prevention service, or
individual recommendation. The concepts may show fictional hold, repeat,
reschedule, reconsider, or no-change choices only so we can learn whether the
explanation and approval approach is understandable and useful.

## What participation involves

The session is expected to take approximately 45 minutes. You may be asked to:

- describe how you selected or use a marathon plan;
- describe a time when real life disrupted that plan;
- react to a fictional readiness result and disruption example;
- explain what you believe each concept is asking you to do; and
- discuss whether the proposed service and price would be useful to you.

You may skip any question, take a break, or stop the session at any time. There
is no penalty for declining, stopping, or choosing not to place a later
reservation. This research session is not recorded.

## Information collected

Marathoner will collect the minimum information needed to conduct and evaluate
the research: your name and email, broad time zone and scheduling information,
adult and United States eligibility confirmations, broad race and plan status,
your responses to the tasks, consent choices, and facilitator notes.

Please do not send medical records, precise location or GPS history, payment
card details, account passwords, government identification, or another
person's information. You choose how much health or training context to share.
Unnecessary clinical detail will not be copied into the research notes.

Private records are stored in an access-restricted Marathoner Google Workspace.
They are not stored in the public GitHub repository and are not pasted into AI
tools. Research findings may be published only as aggregate counts, coarse
source categories, paraphrased themes, and counterexamples that do not
reasonably identify you. This sprint does not publish verbatim participant
quotes.

## Retention and deletion

Contact details, consent, scheduling records, raw notes, and coded evidence are
deleted 90 days after the commercial decision and no later than 12 months after
collection. The session is not audio recorded, video recorded, transcribed, or
processed by an automated note-taking service.

You may withdraw by stopping the session or emailing
`kevin@marathonerapp.com` with the subject **Withdraw from Marathoner
research**. You do not need to give a reason. Marathoner will acknowledge the
request within two business days and complete deletion of your private research
records within seven calendar days.

Withdrawal cannot reverse a non-identifying aggregate already published. If
you later make a reservation, Marathoner may also need to retain a minimum
payment or refund record for financial, tax, dispute, or legal obligations;
that separate reservation notice will explain the terms before payment.

## No recording

Marathoner does not record these sessions. The facilitator takes private notes.
Any future proposal to record requires new participant-facing information and
a new affirmative choice; this consent cannot be reused to authorize it.

## Separate reservation decision

Research consent does not authorize a charge. Some qualified participants may
later be shown the same offer for a $99 fixed-duration First Marathon Journey
and asked whether they want to place a $25 fully refundable reservation that
would be credited toward that price. The checkout, refund terms, expiration,
and seller identity will be shown before payment.

Declining or refunding a reservation does not affect this research session. A
reservation does not create an app account, provide a training recommendation,
or enroll you in a pilot.

## Separate pilot decision

This consent does not enroll you in a concierge or product beta. Any later
pilot requires a new description and consent that explains its exact behavior,
data use, support, withdrawal, safety boundary, and qualified-review status.

## Risks and contact

The main foreseeable risks are discomfort discussing an uncertain training
experience and loss of confidentiality. You may skip a topic or stop. Marathoner
reduces confidentiality risk through minimum collection, random research IDs,
restricted access, short retention, and aggregate public reporting, but no
online system can be guaranteed risk-free.

For research, privacy, withdrawal, or support questions, contact
`kevin@marathonerapp.com`. Do not send passwords, card details, or unnecessary
health information by email.

## Required consent fields

The private Form must collect these as separate required fields:

1. **Age:** “I confirm that I am at least 18 years old.” — Yes / No
2. **Location:** “I confirm that I currently live in the United States.” — Yes /
   No
3. **Research consent:** “I have read the information above, had the opportunity
   to ask questions, and voluntarily agree to this commercial research session.”
   — Yes / No
4. **No-recording acknowledgment:** “I understand that this session will not be
   recorded or transcribed.” — Yes / No
5. **Consent version:** displayed value matching this template
6. **Consent time:** the Form submission timestamp

A “No” answer to age, location, or research consent ends the Form without
collecting task responses. Reservation and pilot consent must not appear as
required research-consent fields.
